About Me

I Don't Just Learn It.
I Build It.

I'm Tegan Rogers — a CS student wrapping up my Bachelor's degree while simultaneously running Evergreen Digital, a web development agency I built from zero to serve real local businesses across the Pacific Northwest.

My path didn't start behind a keyboard. I spent 2.5 years in the restaurant industry — working my way from server to bartender to shift lead — learning how to read people, stay calm under pressure, and make every interaction count. That took me to Nissan of Everett, where I've spent over a year as a Product Specialist, guiding customers through one of the biggest purchases of their lives.

Now I combine both worlds. I write code that solves real business problems, and I can sit across the table from a client, understand what they actually need, and go build something that grows their revenue. That combination is rare — and that's exactly the point.

Sales Philosophy

How I Sell

The approach that drives 15+ cars a month — and why it works in any industry.

"
People don't want to be sold to. They want someone who listens, understands what they actually need, and guides them to a decision they feel confident about. That's not a sales tactic — it's just respect. And it's what makes customers come back and send their friends.
— Tegan Rogers, Product Specialist
01

Comfort Before Commitment

A tense customer is a customer who walks. I lead with genuine conversation — no pressure, no scripts — until they feel like they're talking to someone on their side.

02

Understand Before You Pitch

I ask more questions than I give answers. Knowing someone's commute, their family size, their budget anxiety — that's what lets me recommend the right vehicle instead of the most expensive one.

03

Guide, Don't Push

My job isn't to close the deal — it's to remove every reason not to. When a customer genuinely believes they're making the right choice, the close takes care of itself.

04

The Sale Isn't the Finish Line

Every customer is a long-term relationship. The referrals, the reviews, the repeat buyers — those come from the experience I gave them, not the paperwork they signed.

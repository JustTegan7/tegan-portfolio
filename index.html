About Me

People First.
Then I Build It.

I'm Tegan Rogers. I co-founded Evergreen Digital, a subscription web agency for local businesses in Snohomish County and the Seattle area, where I lead operations and client onboarding. I also designed and launched our two software products, Grove and Juniper.

My path started with people. I led front-end project teams as a Team Lead intern at Super Purposes, then spent almost two years as a Closing Supervisor at Blazing Onion, running the closing team and building a base of regulars behind the bar. Since March 2025 I've been a Product Specialist at Nissan of Everett, owning every deal from first conversation to financing to the questions that come up months later.

That's the job I want next: helping customers get real value from great software. I can sit across from a customer, understand what they actually need, and then explain exactly how the product solves it, or go build the fix myself. Customer success, account management, implementation, or solutions, I'm ready.

How I Work

How I Take Care of Customers

The approach behind 15+ cars a month and 50+ five-star reviews, and exactly how I'd run a book of software customers.

"
People don't want to be sold to. They want someone who listens, understands what they actually need, and helps them make a decision they feel good about. Then they want that person to still pick up the phone a month later. That's what keeps customers and brings in their friends.
Tegan Rogers
01

Comfort Before Commitment

I lead with real conversation, no pressure and no scripts, until the customer feels like I'm on their side. In software, that's a kickoff call where they actually open up.

02

Understand Before You Pitch

I ask more questions than I answer. Knowing their goals and worries lets me recommend what fits, not what's most expensive. That's discovery and success planning.

03

Guide, Don't Push

My job is to remove every reason not to move forward. When customers believe in the decision, adoption takes care of itself.

04

The Sale Isn't the Finish Line

I follow up, answer day-to-day questions, and check in long after delivery. Referrals, reviews, and renewals come from that experience.
